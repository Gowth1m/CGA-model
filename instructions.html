How to Use the Showroom

🖱️ Mouse & Touch Controls

On desktop, simply move your mouse around the screen to experience a dynamic 3D parallax effect. On mobile devices, swipe and drag your finger across the screen to rotate and inspect the vehicle.

✋ AI Hand Tracking

Click the "Enable Hand Tracking" button to use your webcam for an immersive, touch-free experience. (Make sure to allow camera permissions when prompted!)

🤏 Pinch to Rotate & Zoom

While hand tracking is active, pinch your thumb and index finger together. While holding the pinch, move your hand left, right, up, or down to rotate the car in the showroom. Bring your pinched fingers close to the camera to zoom out, and pull them further away to zoom in.
